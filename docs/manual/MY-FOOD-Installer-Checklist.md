# MY FOOD — Installer Checklist

Restaurant: **Chefelisha Restaurant** · MY FOOD address: **www.chefelisha.cc** · Full instructions: *MY FOOD Installation & Operations Manual* (section numbers shown as §).

Download the manual, this checklist and the Device Register in MY FOOD: **Set-up guide** → **Manual and checklists**.

Never write passwords, PINs or pairing codes on this sheet.

## 1. Before you start (§5)

- ☐ Internet works; a PC in the restaurant opens www.chefelisha.cc
- ☐ Mode decided with the owner: ☐ Online mode (MY FOOD Printing) ☐ Hub mode (MY FOOD Hub) (§2)
- ☐ Every PC/tablet has Chrome or Edge
- ☐ Printers: paper loaded, powered, self-test page printed, IP address noted
- ☐ Owner account exists and the owner can sign in (§6)
- ☐ Menu, taxes, floor and stations entered, or a date is agreed (§6.4–6.7)
- ☐ Staff added; each has a starting PIN given privately (§7)

## 2. Devices & printing: records (§8)

- ☐ A record for every device, named by the standard (§18): POS-NN, KITCHEN-NN, DRINKS-NN, CUSTOMER-DISPLAY-NN, PRINT-AGENT-NN, HUB-NN, KITCHEN-PRINTER-NN, RECEIPT-PRINTER-NN
- ☐ Kitchen screens have their **Station**; tills have their **Receipt printer**

## 3. Printing (§13–14)

- ☐ Online mode: **MY FOOD Printing** installed on the printing PC (Windows warning: **More info → Run anyway**)
- ☐ Its code entered on the **PRINT-AGENT-01** row only → it shows **Printing is on · PRINT-AGENT-01**
- ☐ Each printer: **Edit** → connection, IP / Windows printer name, paper width, **Printed by**, backup
- ☐ **Print test page** in MY FOOD Printing works for every printer
- ☐ **Test print** in Devices & printing reports **printed**
- ☐ Stations & routing: every station has its printer/screen (**Main** / **Extra copy** / **Backup**)
- ☐ The Devices & printing panel says **Printing is on**

## 4. Hub (§12), Hub mode only

- ☐ Hub installed on an always-on, cabled PC; starts with Windows
- ☐ Hub code entered on the **MY FOOD Hub** row → **Connected**
- ☐ **Run branch from hub** done; tills open the Hub's address
- ☐ Hub restart test: devices reconnect by themselves

## 6. End-to-end test (§11.2)

- ☐ Staff PIN sign-in on each till (first sign-in: they choose their own PIN)
- ☐ Takeaway test order sent → kitchen screen shows it with *Sent by*
- ☐ Kitchen ticket printed at the right station
- ☐ **Print bill** → **BILL - NOT PAID** printed
- ☐ Cash payment with change; MoMo and card recorded (with reference)
- ☐ **Print receipt** printed on the till's receipt printer
- ☐ Kitchen **START → READY** → customer display shows it under **Ready**
- ☐ **Picked up / Served** → order **Completed**
- ☐ Dual-screen till: **Customer screen** opens on the second monitor (if fitted)
- ☐ Product with a recipe sold → stock went down
- ☐ Register opened and closed; closing report exported as PDF
- ☐ **Reports → End of Day** shows the test; PDF / Excel / CSV downloaded
- ☐ Printer failure test: printer off → order → printer on → ticket prints **once**
- ☐ Internet test: unplug → **Retry send** works after reconnecting (Hub mode: the till keeps working)
- ☐ Test orders cancelled; owner shown the Activity page

## 7. Handover (§37)

- ☐ Owner and managers shown: sign-in, Devices & printing, Staff, Reports, Register
- ☐ Quick Reference card placed at each till
- ☐ Device register completed (MY-FOOD-Device-Register.xlsx)
- ☐ Contacts filled in (§39)

> Physical hardware was not available when this manual was written. These on-site tests are the first real-hardware verification, so do all of them.
